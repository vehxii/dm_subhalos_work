This repository contains most work from the summer of 2025 with Dr. Ethan O. Nadler, postdoc Dr. Sandip Roy, and undergraduate student Roxanne Lai (all at UC San Diego) on a project studying the connection between subhalo counts and dark matter mass accretion. "DM SUBHALOS WORK" Notebooks are from the summer, and "DM tinkering" Notebooks are from the 2025-2026 school year.
